Your Home Information, Organized, Verified, And Always Within Reach

Your home is one of your most important investments. Keeping track of its records shouldn’t feel overwhelming. Windor helps homeowners store, verify, and access essential property documentation—so nothing gets lost when you need it most.

How Windor Helps Homeowners

Over time, important home documents repair documentation, upgrades and permits become scattered or forgotten. Windor focuses specifically on documentation confirmation and validation, helping homeowners:

  • Keep all property-related documents in one secure, centralized place
  • Ensure records are complete, accurate, and easy to access
  • Track home improvements, repairs, and upgrades over time
  • Maintain a verified history of the home for future needs
  • Helping property owners verify unknown product information

With everything organized and validated, homeowners can avoid last-minute stress when documents are needed for insurance, refinancing, or selling.

Accurate documentation also protects the long-term value of your home. A well-documented property makes it easier to prove improvements, support warranties, and simplify future transactions.

We are not storing reciepts, warranties or anything other that documenting the project information with photos (at this time)

Built for the Way Homeowners Manage Their Homes

Windor is not a home management or maintenance service. Instead, it acts as a specialized verification layer, allowing homeowners to:

Stay organized without managing complex systems

Avoid scrambling for paperwork when it matters most

Keep records aligned with property requirements and history

Maintain a complete file of documents, photos, and notes tied to their home

Frequently Asked Questions

What types of documents can I store in Windor.app? +

Windor is designed for property-related documentation such as warranties, permits, inspection reports, and upgrade details.

Why should I keep track of home documentation? +

Having complete records helps with insurance claims, warranty coverage, refinancing, and resale. Missing documents can lead to delays or lost value.

Is this a home maintenance or repair service? +

No. Windor does not provide maintenance services. It focuses strictly on organizing and verifying your home’s documentation.

Will this help if I decide to sell my home? +

Yes. A fully documented home can make the selling process smoother by giving buyers confidence and reducing back-and-forth during due diligence.

Can I use this for multiple properties? +

Yes. Windor allows homeowners to manage and verify documentation across multiple properties in one place.
