DOCUMENTATION SERVICES

Document Everything That Matters With Clear,
Structured Reporting

Photos alone aren’t enough. Windor transforms project documentation into organized, professional reports that clearly show what was completed, when it was done, and how it was captured. Nothing gets lost, questioned, or overlooked.

WHAT OUR DOCUMENTATION
SERVICES DELIVER

Windor provides structured, photo-backed documentation services designed to create a clear, reliable record of completed exterior projects.
We don’t perform installations or repairs. We focus entirely on capturing and organizing accurate visual documentation you can trust and share.

OUR DOCUMENTATION SERVICES INCLUDE:

  • Roofing project documentation
  • Siding installation tracking
  • Window and door documentation
  • Property upgrade and improvement records
  • Multi-phase project documentation

Our documentation is built to keep every detail organized, accessible, and reliable.

BUILT FOR ORGANIZATION, ACCURACY, AND ACCOUNTABILITY

Our documentation services create a single source of truth so you always
know what was completed, recorded, and verified.

IDEAL FOR:

  • Contractors documenting completed work for clients
  • Property managers tracking improvements across properties
  • Insurance providers maintaining clear project records
  • Manufacturers requiring documentation for product installations
  • Homeowners who want a complete record of their investment

WHAT DOCUMENTATION DOES — AND DOESN'T DO

WHAT WE DO

  • Capture and organize project documentation
  • Provide structured, photo-backed reports
  • Create clear records of completed work

WHAT WE DON'T DO

  • Perform installations or repairs
  • Act as contractors or inspectors
  • Provide licensed inspection or engineering evaluations

This keeps our documentation focused, accurate, and unbiased.
