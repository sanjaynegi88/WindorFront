VERIFICATION SERVICES

Proof Your Installation Was Done Right With Verified,
Photo-Backed Documentation

When roofing, siding, windows, or doors are installed, you need more than a verbal confirmation—you need clear, visual proof. Windor delivers structured verification reports that document completed installations with accuracy and transparency.

WHAT OUR VERIFICATION
SERVICES DELIVER

Windor provides independent verification and documentation of completed exterior installations—without performing any of the work.
We focus on capturing what was installed, how it appears, and confirming completion through organized reporting.

OUR VERIFICATION COVERS:

  • Roofing installations and replacements
  • Siding installation and repairs
  • Window installations
  • Door installations

EVERY VERIFICATION REPORT INCLUDES:

  • Photo-backed documentation of completed work
  • Coverage of key installation areas and components
  • Clear summaries of what was observed
  • Timestamped images for accountability
  • Structured reports ready for sharing or recordkeeping

Our role is simple: document and verify—so you get clear, unbiased insight into completed work.

BUILT FOR CLARITY, NOT COMPLEXITY

We provide objective, third-party verification so you can confidently
understand what has been completed—without added complexity.

IDEAL FOR:

  • Contractors needing proof of completed installations
  • Homeowners wanting reassurance and documentation
  • Insurance providers verifying repair or replacement work
  • Manufacturers confirming proper product installation
  • Property managers tracking upgrades across properties

WHAT VERIFICATION DOES — AND DOESN'T DO

WHAT WE DO

  • Document completed installations
  • Provide photo-backed reporting
  • Verify visible completion of work

WHAT WE DON'T DO

  • Perform installations or repairs
  • Provide contractor services
  • Replace inspections requiring licensed evaluations

This keeps our reporting neutral, focused, and reliable.
