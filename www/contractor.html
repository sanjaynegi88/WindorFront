Contractor Projects Move Faster When Documentation Is Easy And Centralized

Contractors spend hours invested into a home project. It shouldn’t take hours to show and document the end results. Let Windor help ensure your project documentation is complete, accurate, and ready to be accessed by everyone involved for confirmation of your work.

How Windor Helps Contractors

Contractors are often juggling multiple properties, timelines, and stakeholders while documentation requirements continue to grow. Windor focuses specifically on documentation confirmation and validation, helping contractors:

  • Confirm document accuracy and completeness across each project
  • Reduce delays caused by missing or inconsistent paperwork
  • Support compliance with city regulations
  • Create a clear, verifiable record of property-related documentation

By ensuring documentation is properly entered and easily accessible, contractors can move forward with confidence without getting bogged down by time-consuming administrative work.

Accurate documentation plays a critical role in assuring a project moves swiftly and a project history is preserved—making it easier to complete product and labor warranties as well as future upgrades to a home.

Built for the Way Contractors Work

Windor is not a full-service project manager or contractor platform. Instead, it acts as a specialized verification layer, allowing contractors to:

Focus on execution, not paperwork

Avoid bottlenecks tied to documentation

Maintain better alignment with city regulations

Keep projects moving with clean and organized records including documentation, photos, and notes

Our Story

After serving as a Special Forces officer, Frank Serna returned home with a mission—to build a better future for his family. He found success in construction, growing multiple companies in the contractor, windows and doors industry. But along the way, he saw a critical gap: vital home information was scattered, outdated, or lost.

In 2020, he launched the initial Windor.app to solve this problem—creating a centralized platform where contractors, homeowners, insurers, and regulators can access and share essential home data.

Now, with a scalable new version in development, Windor is transforming how homes are documented and maintained.

Because every home is more than an investment—it’s a story.

And with Windor, that story is never lost.

Frequently Asked Questions

What types of documents are stored at Windor.app? +

Windor focuses on property-related documentation such as ownership records, supporting property details, and other documentation-required files tied to project readiness.

How is this different from a full-service platform? +

Windor does not manage projects, contractors, or construction workflows. It strictly handles documentation of a project ready to be reviewed ensuring accuracy and completeness so others can move forward.

Why does documentation matter after finishing a project? +

Inconsistent documentation can lead to delays in payment, rejected approvals, or compliance issues. Accurate reporting ensures everything is complete after work is performed.

Does this help with approvals or project funding? +

Yes. Clear documentation supports smoother approval processes by ensuring required information is complete and trustworthy.

Can this scale across multiple properties or jobs? +

Absolutely. Windor is designed to support contractors managing multiple projects in different properties by standardizing and verifying documentation across projects.
