REPORTING SERVICES

Know Exactly What Work Was Done With Clear,
Verified Reporting

Stop chasing updates, or relying on incomplete documentation. Windor delivers structured, photo-backed reports that clearly show what work has been completed, so you can move forward with confidence.

WHAT OUR REPORTING
SERVICES DELIVER

Windor provides independent, third-party reporting that captures the true status of a construction project or home improvement.
Our reports are designed to give you clarity, proof, and accountability without adding complexity to your workflow.

EVERY REPORT INCLUDES:

  • Detailed summaries of completed work
  • Before-and-after photo documentation
  • Verification of upgrades, repairs, or installations
  • Organized, easy-to-read formats for quick review
  • Timestamped records for accountability and tracking

Accurate reporting is critical for protecting investments and ensuring project transparency, especially when multiple parties are involved.

BUILT FOR REAL-WORLD USE

Whether you're managing one property or an entire portfolio,
our reporting services give you a clear, unbiased view of progress.

IDEAL FOR:

  • Contractors needing proof of completed work
  • Homeowners wanting visibility into project progress
  • Insurance providers validating repairs or claims
  • Manufacturers confirming proper installation
  • Project managers tracking milestones and quality
