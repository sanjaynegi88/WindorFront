Services Built to Strengthen Project Confidence and Compliance

From project photos to structured reporting and complete documentation, Windor delivers the clarity, proof, and accountability of your projects, so you can move forward with confidence.

WHAT WE DO

Windor provides project verification and documentation services designed to eliminate uncertainty and streamline communication. Whether you're managing construction, inspections, or compliance requirements, our services ensure that every step of your project is captured, validated, and clearly reported.

We bridge the gap between work completed and proof delivered, transforming field activity into organized, accessible, and audit-ready records.

Verification Services Offings:

On-Site Verification Reports

We provide an onsite claim breakdown verification where our trained representative verifies each line item of a claim and compares it to what was actually performed, giving the property owner or carrier documented proof of completed work.

Final Verification Report

We provide a detailed report of product verification of materials and codes that provides city inspectors and insurance carriers with documented facts of work performed and installed products.
