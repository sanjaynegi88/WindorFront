Product Documentation That Stays Connected To The Home Long After Installation

Manufacturers invest heavily in product quality and performance. That value shouldn’t be lost after installation. Windor ensures your product documentation is verified, accessible, and tied directly to the property which supports visibility, accountability, and long-term performance.

How Windor Helps Manufacturers

Once products leave the warehouse and are installed, documentation often becomes fragmented or lost between contractors, homeowners, and service providers. Windor focuses specifically on documentation confirmation and validation, helping manufacturers:

  • Ensure product documentation is properly recorded at the property level
  • Reduce warranty disputes caused by missing or incomplete records
  • Support product verification with accurate installation documentation
  • Maintain a clear, traceable record of products tied to specific homes

By connecting documentation directly to the property, manufacturers gain greater confidence that their products are properly represented and supported in the field.

Accurate documentation also strengthens warranty programs, improves customer satisfaction, and provides a reliable foundation for future service, replacement, or upgrades.

Built for the Way Manufacturers Support Their Products

Windor is not a product management or supply chain platform. Instead, it acts as a specialized verification layer, allowing manufacturers to:

Extend visibility beyond the point of sale

Avoid gaps in documentation after installation

Support warranty validation with verified records

Maintain organized documentation including product details, photos, and installation notes

Our Story

After serving as a Special Forces officer, Frank Serna returned home with a mission—to build a better future for his family. He found success in construction, growing multiple companies in the contractor, windows and doors industry. But along the way, he saw a critical gap: vital home information was scattered, outdated, or lost.

In 2020, he launched the initial Windor.app to solve this problem—creating a centralized platform where contractors, homeowners, insurers, and regulators can access and share essential home data.

Now, with a scalable new version in development, Windor is transforming how homes are documented and maintained.

Because every home is more than an investment—it’s a story.

And with Windor, that story is never lost.

Frequently Asked Questions

What types of product information can be stored in Windor.app? +

Windor supports documentation such as product specifications, installation records, warranties, and supporting materials tied directly to a property.

How is this different from internal product tracking systems? +

Windor does not replace internal systems. It ensures that product documentation is verified and properly connected to the property after installation.

Why is post-installation documentation important? +

Missing or inaccurate documentation can lead to warranty disputes, service delays, and reduced trust. Verified records ensure clarity for all parties involved.

Does this help with warranty claims? +

Yes. Windor provides a clear, verified record of installation and product details, helping streamline warranty validation and reduce disputes.

Can this scale across multiple products and locations? +

Absolutely. Windor is designed to support manufacturers across multiple product lines and properties by standardizing and verifying documentation at scale.
