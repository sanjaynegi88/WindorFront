Better Claims Start With Verified Property Documentation

Insurance decisions depend on accurate information, but property documentation is often incomplete or inconsistent. Windor helps ensure records are verified, accessible, and tied directly to the home, giving insurers greater confidence at every stage of the policy and claims process.

How Windor Helps Insurance Companies

From underwriting to claims, insurers rely on clear property data, but documentation is frequently scattered across homeowners, contractors, and third parties. Windor focuses specifically on documentation confirmation and validation, helping insurance companies:

  • Access complete, verified property documentation when it matters most
  • Reduce delays caused by missing or inconsistent records during claims
  • Improve underwriting confidence with accurate property histories
  • Create a reliable, centralized record of property-related documentation

By ensuring documentation is accurate and readily available, insurers can streamline workflows, reduce uncertainty, and make more informed decisions.

Accurate documentation also helps minimize disputes, accelerate claims resolution, and improve the overall policyholder experience.

Built for the Way Insurance Companies Operate

Windor is not a claims management or policy administration system. Instead, it acts as a specialized verification layer, allowing insurers to:

Make decisions based on verified, not assumed, property data

Avoid delays tied to incomplete or missing documentation

Strengthen alignment between underwriting and claims teams

Maintain organized records including documentation, photos, and property notes

Our Story

After serving as a Special Forces officer, Frank Serna returned home with a mission—to build a better future for his family. He found success in construction, growing multiple companies in the contractor, windows and doors industry. But along the way, he saw a critical gap: vital home information was scattered, outdated, or lost.

In 2020, he launched the initial Windor.app to solve this problem—creating a centralized platform where contractors, homeowners, insurers, and regulators can access and share essential home data.

Now, with a scalable new version in development, Windor is transforming how homes are documented and maintained.

Because every home is more than an investment—it’s a story.

And with Windor, that story is never lost.

Frequently Asked Questions

What types of documentation are available through Windor.app? +

Windor focuses on property-related documentation such as inspection records, permits, repair history, product installations, and other supporting materials tied to a home.

How is this different from existing claims or underwriting systems? +

Windor does not replace internal systems. It ensures that property documentation is verified, complete, and accessible to support better decision-making.

Why is verified documentation important for insurers? +

Incomplete or inaccurate records can delay claims, increase risk exposure, and lead to disputes. Verified documentation provides clarity and confidence across processes.

Does this help speed up claims? +

Yes. Having access to complete, validated documentation reduces back-and-forth and helps claims teams move faster with greater accuracy.

Can this scale across multiple policies and properties? +

Absolutely. Windor is designed to support insurers managing large portfolios by standardizing and verifying documentation across properties.
